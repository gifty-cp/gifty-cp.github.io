Hospitality Revenue Analytics

Hotel Booking Cancellations & Revenue Protection

1. Business Problem

The hotel partner was experiencing an alarming 37% overall cancellation rate, leading to empty rooms that could not be re-sold on short notice and an estimated annual revenue loss exceeding \$140,000.

2. Data Cleaning & Methodology (SQL)

Loaded 119,390 records across City Hotel and Resort Hotel properties. Handled missing agent and company IDs, filtered out erroneous 0-guest bookings, and engineered cohorts for lead-time brackets (0-30d, 31-90d, 91-180d, 180d+). 

WITH lead_cohorts AS (
  SELECT 
    CASE 
      WHEN lead_time <= 30 THEN '0-30 days'
      WHEN lead_time <= 90 THEN '31-90 days'
      ELSE '90+ days'
    END AS lead_bracket,
    is_canceled,
    adr * (stays_in_week_nights + stays_in_weekend_nights) AS est_revenue
  FROM hotel_bookings
)
SELECT 
  lead_bracket,
  COUNT(*) AS total_bookings,
  ROUND(AVG(is_canceled) * 100, 2) AS cancellation_pct
FROM lead_cohorts
GROUP BY lead_bracket;

3. Key Analytical Insights

  • Bookings made >90 days in advance had a 58.4% cancellation rate compared to just 14.1% for reservations made within 14 days.
  • Non-refundable bookings accounted for only 12% of total volume but suffered from high dispute friction.
  • Online Travel Agencies (OTAs) had double the cancellation rate of direct telephone or brand website bookings.

4. Executive Recommendation & Impact

Advised management to replace the rigid binary (free cancellation vs. 100% non-refundable) with a tiered 15% deposit policy for any booking >60 days out. Initial pilot modeling showed this retained 82% of booking volume while eliminating speculative reservations, protecting an estimated \$42,000 annually.
