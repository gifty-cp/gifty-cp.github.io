Case Study · Customer Complaints Analytics

Complaints Dashboard

Commonwealth Bank Customer Complaints Register & SLA Performance (2011–2020)

Dataset: 467 complaints (2011–2020) Open Live Dashboard View the Files

1. Business Question

A complaint can receive a response on time, but does that mean the customer’s concern has been resolved?

2. What the Dashboard Explores

  • Total complaints, timely responses, disputes, relief rates and cases still in progress
  • How complaints were submitted, including web, referral and phone
  • Quarterly complaint trends and annual breakdowns by product
  • Complaint volumes across Australian states
  • Interactive filters by product, issue and year

3. Finding

94.4% of complaints (441 of 467) received a timely response, yet 20.6% (96) were still escalated to a formal dispute, so on-time handling didn’t always mean the customer felt resolved.

4. Why This Question Matters to Me

I relate to it from hospitality, where responding quickly and making sure a guest feels heard both matter. Claude AI helped me build the dashboard, while I practised reading charts, understanding the measures, and thinking about what the numbers can, and cannot, tell us.
