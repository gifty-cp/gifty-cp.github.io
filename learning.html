INDEX + MATCH + MAX: finding the top performer

My BIG W dashboard has KPI cards for the top manager and top buyer. I used this formula next to each PivotTable to pull out the name of whoever had the highest sales, so the card updates on its own. 

=INDEX(A5:A14,
   MATCH(MAX(D5:D14), D5:D14, 0))

MAX(D5:D14)Finds the highest sales total148,593
MATCH(148593, D5:D14, 0)Finds which position that total is in (0 = exact match)1

Same formula on the buyers PivotTable. Jarrah beat Arjun by just $10, a reminder that a “top” label can hide a near tie.

Watch out with slicers: filtering the dashboard can leave fewer than 10 people in the PivotTable. The Grand Total row then moves up into A5:A14 and, being the biggest number, wins the MAX, so the card shows “Grand Total”. For example, setting the State slicer to NSW leaves only 5 managers.

Fix: use MAXIFS so the Grand Total row is ignored:

=INDEX(A5:A14,
   MATCH(MAXIFS(D5:D14, A5:A14, "<>Grand Total"),
         D5:D14, 0))
