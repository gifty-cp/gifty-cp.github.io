Excel · Formula

Turning text into real dates

Imported dates sometimes arrive as text in month/day/year order, such as 03/15/2024. Excel can’t sort, filter or group text dates properly, so I rebuild each one as a real date: 

=DATE(VALUE(RIGHT(Source!A3,4)),
      VALUE(LEFT(Source!A3,2)),
      VALUE(MID(Source!A3,4,2)))
Part What it does 03/15/2024 →
RIGHT(A3,4) Takes the last 4 characters: the year "2024"
LEFT(A3,2) Takes the first 2 characters: the month "03"
MID(A3,4,2) Takes 2 characters starting at the 4th: the day "15"
VALUE(…) Turns each piece of text into a number 2024, 3, 15
DATE(y, m, d) Combines year, month and day into a real date 15 Mar 2024
Watch out: it only works when every value has the same layout, two-digit month and day plus a four-digit year. A value like 3/5/2024 would return an error.

Summarising data with PivotTables

A PivotTable turns thousands of rows into a short summary, like complaints per year or totals per product, without writing a single formula.
