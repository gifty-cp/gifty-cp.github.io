How the ranking works

Hours watched = episodes watched × episode length, per subscriber per show. Every subscriber watched some of all nine shows, so the signal is how much, not whether.

For each pair of shows we take the Pearson correlation of hours across the 2,000 subscribers. A candidate's match score is its average correlation with the shows you picked. "Big fans" are subscribers above the median hours for a show; the fan-overlap figure is the share of your picks' big fans who are also big fans of the candidate (50% is what you'd expect by chance).

Correlation shows which shows are watched together. It does not prove one causes interest in the other, and these are viewing patterns for this sample only.
