Pearson correlation of customer spend by category

Method: for each customer we add up their spend (quantity × unit price) in every category across all their orders. Each cell is the Pearson correlation between two categories across all customers: +1 means customers who spend more on one also spend proportionally more on the other, 0 means no linear relationship, and negative values mean spend moves in opposite directions. Categories are listed in business order (A–J), not alphabetically.
